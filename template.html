        Definitions and sources

        Prices and fundamentals come from Yahoo Finance (chart and quoteSummary endpoints) at each refresh: market cap, enterprise value, trailing EV/EBITDA, forward P/E, latest-quarter revenue growth, gross margin, analyst mean target and next earnings date. Non-US figures are converted to US dollars at the refresh-time USD/KRW, USD/JPY and USD/TWD rates shown in the status chips.

        Returns are computed from daily closes: 1M, 3M and 12M use the close nearest that many calendar days before the latest close; YTD uses the last close of the prior calendar year. Off high is the latest close against the highest close in the trailing 12 months.

        Relative performance indexes each series to 100 at its first close inside the chosen window, so lines compare percentage moves rather than price levels.

        Lumentum deal monitor: implied value per LITE share = 4.5 x MRVL price. The spread is LITE's price against that implied value; a negative number means LITE trades below what the fixed ratio pays. MRVL's move since the unaffected date uses its 28 Oct 2026 close.

        Curated figures (results, targets, deal terms, bull and bear points) are hand-maintained in curated.json with a source link on each item and an as-of date in the header chip. Secondary sources are labelled as such.

        Not investment advice. Public data only; check primary filings before relying on any figure.
